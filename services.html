What it does

Four jobs, done on every call.

Each receptionist is set up for one business, from its hours and services to the calls that need a person.

01 · Answer

Picks up in your business name.

  • After hours, overflow, or every call first
  • Answers FAQs: hours, location, services, forms
  • Captures insurance or job details
  • Discloses it is an AI assistant

02 · Book

Writes straight into your system.

  • Checks live availability
  • Books, reschedules and cancels
  • Reads every booking back to the caller
  • Never double-books on a retry

03 · Hand off

Gets a person on the line when it matters.

  • Live transfer to your team or on-call number
  • Emergencies directed to 911 or your emergency line
  • A message and summary when nobody picks up

04 · Report

Shows your team what happened.

  • An alert to staff after every call
  • A dashboard of calls, bookings and requests
  • Clear notes for anything that needs follow-up
