### Overview
Vocorix builds practical voice AI and workflow automation around the way your team works. We help connect customer conversations to concrete next steps—from answering inbound questions and capturing intent to routing enquiries, coordinating appointments, updating CRMs, and keeping systems in sync.

We start with the workflow, not the hype. Every solution is shaped around your business rules, integration needs, and the points where a person should take over. The goal is straightforward: reduce repetitive work, make follow-through more reliable, and give your team the context it needs to act.

Capabilities include:
• Voice AI and call handling
• Workflow orchestration and lead routing
• CRM, calendar, and system integrations
• Conversation design, exception handling, and human handoffs

Learn more at https://vocorix.com/

## Launch checklist
- Use the mark-only asset for the LinkedIn profile image; use the full wordmark on the cover.
- Keep logo, site, LinkedIn copy, and social graphics aligned to the palette above.
- Add only confirmed organization details (location, industry, founding year, size, contact email) in LinkedIn's structured fields.
- Add the LinkedIn company-page URL back to the website only after the page exists.
- Claim page admin access with the real company representative account; LinkedIn verification badges are granted by LinkedIn and cannot be self-assigned.
