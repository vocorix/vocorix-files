Questions

What people usually ask.

Anything else, send it through the form below.

Does it sound like a robot?

It uses natural voices and speaks in your business name. It also tells callers it is an AI assistant, so there are no surprises.

Does it really book into our software?

Yes. It checks live availability and writes the appointment into your PMS, CRM or calendar, then reads it back to the caller. If the system can't be reached, it takes a message for your team instead of promising a slot.

What if a caller wants a person?

It transfers the call live to your team or on-call number. If no one picks up, it takes a message and sends your team a summary.

Is patient data handled safely?

For healthcare clients we sign a Business Associate Agreement and build only on services that do the same. Personal details are kept out of logs and each client's data is kept separate.

How long does setup take?

Usually within 10 business days of getting access to your systems. You fill in a short setup form and we handle the rest.

Can our agency offer it to clients?

Yes. It can run under your brand, with simple wholesale pricing per client. You set the retail price and keep the client relationship.
