01 · A real call

What actually happens on the phone.

Every receptionist follows the practice's own hours, scheduling rules and escalation rules. Here is a typical after-hours call, with what the system does at each step.

Caller

Hi, I chipped a tooth. Can I get in tomorrow?

Answered in the practice's name, with an AI disclosure.
Receptionist

I'm sorry to hear that. Are you already a patient with us?

Finds the patient record, or creates a new one.
Receptionist

Tomorrow is full, but I have Thursday at 9:00 AM with Dr. Patel. Would that work?

Live availability from the PMS, within the practice's rules.
Caller

Yes, book it. Can I also talk to someone about the cost?

Books, then reads the time back before ending.
Receptionist

You're booked for Thursday at 9:00 AM. I'll connect you to the on-call line now.

Live transfer. If nobody answers, it takes a message and alerts the team.

04 · Questions

Before you ask.

Will callers know it's AI?

Yes. It says so at the start of every call. It uses a natural voice and speaks in your business name.

What if it can't reach our system?

It takes the request and sends it to your team to confirm. It never promises a slot that doesn't exist.

Do we have to change our phone number?

No. You keep your number and forward calls to the receptionist during the hours you choose.

Can we try it first?

Yes. Start with one month on one or two locations at the normal price, with no commitment after it.
