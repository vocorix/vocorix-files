LinkedIn company overview

Vocorix builds practical voice AI and workflow automation around the way your team works. We help connect customer conversations to concrete next steps—from answering inbound questions and capturing intent to routing enquiries, coordinating appointments, updating CRMs, and keeping systems in sync. We start with the workflow, not the hype. Every solution is shaped around your business rules, integration needs, and the points where a person should take over. The goal is straightforward: reduce repetitive work, make follow-through more reliable, and give your team the context it needs to act. Our capabilities include voice AI and call handling, workflow orchestration and lead routing, CRM and system integrations, and conversation design with clear exception handling and human handoffs.
