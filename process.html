How it works

From one form to live calls in about two weeks.

You tell us how your office runs. We connect your systems, test the receptionist on your real schedule, and switch calls over when you're happy.

Day 1

Fill in the setup form

Hours, services, providers, appointment types, FAQs and who should take transferred calls.

Days 2–5

Connect your systems

Your PMS, CRM or calendar is connected so the receptionist sees real availability.

Days 6–9

Test on your real schedule

Test calls cover bookings, changes, transfers, emergencies and what happens if a system is down. You review the results.

By day 10

Switch calls over

You forward calls during the hours you choose. You keep your number.

Ongoing

Monitored and adjusted

We watch for problems and update hours, FAQs and rules as your office changes.

Ground rules

How we build it.

01

Your rules come first.

The receptionist only books what your scheduling rules allow and only answers what you've approved.

02

A person is always reachable.

Callers can ask for a person at any point, and anything clinical or unusual goes to your team.

03

Failures fail safe.

If a system can't be reached, the call becomes a message for staff, never a false booking.
