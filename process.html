The delivery process

Four clear steps from idea to launch.

We keep the first version small, test it with real examples, and agree on how we will judge the results.

01

Map the process

Understand entry points, decisions, systems, exceptions, and what a good outcome looks like.

02

Design the handoffs

Plan what the agent should say, which systems it should update, and what happens when a person needs to step in.

03

Build and test

We test normal calls along with interruptions, missing information, and times when a connected tool does not work.

04

Launch and refine

We review what happened, fix the rough edges, and leave clear notes so your team can manage it.

The principles

Useful automation has boundaries.

We write down the rules so your team can check what happened and adjust the workflow later.

01

People can step in

Calls and tasks can be routed to a person when judgement, empathy, or special handling is needed.

02

Notes go where they belong

We decide where call summaries and next steps should go, so important details are easy to find.

03

We plan for things to go wrong

If a call is missed, information is missing, or a connection fails, the workflow needs a clear next step.

04

Your team can see what happened

Clear notes help your team see what the automation did and why.
